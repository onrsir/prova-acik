Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your Prova account and all your data at any time.

In the app (recommended, immediate)
  1. Open Prova and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete all my data and confirm.
Your analyses, chats, scenarios, purchase records, linked account details and remaining credits are permanently deleted.
If you can’t access the app
Send an email with the subject “account deletion” to oonursir@gmail.com and include the Apple/Google email linked to your account. Your request will be completed within 30 days.

Deleted data cannot be recovered. Server backups are cleared automatically within 14 days at the latest.
