The service

Prova is a trial tool in which ideas are simulated with AI agents. The generated content is fictional and for informational purposes only; it is not a substitute for professional advice.

Unacceptable use

Ideas that are illegal, harm others, contain hate or target real people cannot be rehearsed; such requests are rejected automatically and the credit spent is refunded.

Credits and purchases

Credits are a consumable digital product and can only be used to start analyses in this app. Each analysis uses 1 credit; no free trial is offered. Credits are refunded automatically for failed analyses. Store refunds are subject to Apple’s and Google’s own policies. If you don’t link your account, credits belong to the guest account on that device.

Responsibility

Users are responsible for any decisions made based on simulation results. Scores and forecasts are a rehearsal of probabilities, not a guarantee.
