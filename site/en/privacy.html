Privacy policy

Last updated: 25.10.2026. This text also serves as the information notice required under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

In short

Prova does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, phone number or address. It shows no ads in the app. Only if you allow it, limited data is shared with Google, Meta and TikTok to measure our Google, Instagram/Facebook and TikTok ads (see Ad measurement below). You can permanently delete all your data at any time in the app via Profile > Delete all my data.

Data controller

Onur Sir · oonursir@gmail.com

Data we collect and why

  • A random device-specific identifier — to recognize your guest account.
  • Apple / Google account ID and email (optional) — if you link your account, to keep your credits and analyses across devices.
  • The ideas you write, analysis results, your agent chats and scenarios — to provide the service and show results to you again.
  • Purchase records (store transaction ID, product) — to verify credits. Your card details never reach us.
  • Notification token — to notify you when an analysis is finished (you can turn notifications off).
  • Ad measurement data (only if you allow it) — advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Google advertising ID on Android), app instance ID (Google Analytics), device and app information (model, operating system, app version, IP address and the approximate location derived from it), in-app events (install/open, watching a demo, payment screen, starting an analysis, linking an account) and purchase events (product, amount, currency, store transaction ID): to measure which ad led to an install and a purchase. Your ideas, analyses and chats are never shared. If you linked your account, your email is used only on iPhone, for Google's on-device conversion measurement; it does not leave the device in a way that identifies you.

Services your data is shared with

  • Google Gemini API — your idea and the simulation content are sent to the AI model (paid API; content is not used for model training).
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / Overpass and Wikipedia — only the location and business type in your idea are queried.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — sending notifications.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — purchases and sign-in.
  • RevenueCat — verifying in-app purchases with the store and keeping their records (anonymous user ID, product, transaction ID).
  • Google Cloud — the infrastructure hosting our servers (USA, us-central1).
  • Google (Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Ads) — only if you allow it: measuring our app-install ads (advertising identifier, app instance ID, approximate location, in-app events, purchase events).
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook / Instagram) — only if you allow it: measuring our app-install ads (advertising identifier, in-app events, purchase events).
  • TikTok (TikTok for Business) — only if you allow it: measuring our app-install ads (advertising identifier, in-app events, purchase events).

Because some of these services are located outside Türkiye, data may be transferred abroad; any transfer is limited to the purposes above. Google, Meta and TikTok process data outside Türkiye (for example in the USA, the European Union and Singapore); this transfer is based on the explicit consent you give for ad measurement.

Ad measurement and your consent

We promote Prova with app-install ads on Google (Search, YouTube and Google's other ad networks), Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. The app asks for your permission once, at the end of your first demo or before the first payment screen (on iPhone, Apple's "Tracking" permission). If you allow it, Google's, Meta's and TikTok's own measurement software (SDKs) receive the data above to measure which ads work; these companies also process the data under their own privacy policies (Google, Meta, TikTok).

If you don't allow it, no events or identifiers are sent to Google, Meta or TikTok; the Google Analytics software collects nothing until you allow it. On iPhone, only Apple's SKAdNetwork reports installs that came from an ad to the ad network, in aggregate and without identifiers. On Android, Meta's software may fetch its configuration from Meta when the app opens; no advertising identifier or in-app event is sent at that point.

You can withdraw your consent at any time: turn off Profile > Legal > Ad measurement in the app; on iPhone you can also turn Prova off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads. Withdrawing does not affect measurement done before that.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. You can delete a single analysis from the My analyses screen in the app, and all your data via Profile > Delete all my data; deletion cannot be undone and your remaining credits are deleted as well. Server backups are cleared automatically within 14 days.

Your rights

For requests under Article 11 of the KVKK (access to information, correction, deletion, objection), you can write to oonursir@gmail.com.

Children

Prova is not designed for children under 13.
