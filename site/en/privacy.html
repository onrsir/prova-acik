Privacy policy

Last updated: 23.10.2026. This text also serves as the information notice required under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

In short

Prova does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, phone number or address. It does not use advertising or tracking. You can permanently delete all your data at any time in the app via Profile > Delete all my data.

Data controller

Onur Sir · oonursir@gmail.com

Data we collect and why

  • A random device-specific identifier — to recognize your guest account.
  • Apple / Google account ID and email (optional) — if you link your account, to keep your credits and analyses across devices.
  • The ideas you write, analysis results, your agent chats and scenarios — to provide the service and show results to you again.
  • Purchase records (store transaction ID, product) — to verify credits. Your card details never reach us.
  • Notification token — to notify you when an analysis is finished (you can turn notifications off).

Services your data is shared with

  • Google Gemini API — your idea and the simulation content are sent to the AI model (paid API; content is not used for model training).
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / Overpass and Wikipedia — only the location and business type in your idea are queried.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — sending notifications.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — purchases and sign-in.
  • RevenueCat — verifying in-app purchases with the store and keeping their records (anonymous user ID, product, transaction ID).
  • Oracle Cloud — the infrastructure hosting our servers.

Because some of these services are located outside Türkiye, data may be transferred abroad; any transfer is limited to the purposes above.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. You can delete a single analysis from the My analyses screen in the app, and all your data via Profile > Delete all my data; deletion cannot be undone and your remaining credits are deleted as well. Server backups are cleared automatically within 14 days.

Your rights

For requests under Article 11 of the KVKK (access to information, correction, deletion, objection), you can write to oonursir@gmail.com.

Children

Prova is not designed for children under 13.
