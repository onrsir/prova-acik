FAQ

Is there a free trial?
No. You can see what Prova does by watching the ready-made demos in the app; for your own idea, each analysis uses 1 credit (packs of 1 and 3 credits).
Do I lose my credit if an analysis fails?
No. If an analysis can’t be completed, the credit spent is refunded automatically.
How do I keep my credits safe?
In the app, link your Apple or Google account under Profile > Protect your account. Even if you change phones, your analyses and credits come back when you sign in with the same account.
Are the agents real people?
No. Agents are fictional people generated from local data and your idea. Results are a rehearsal; they are not investment, legal or financial advice.
Purchase refunds
Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies: reportaproblem.apple.com · Google Play refunds.
